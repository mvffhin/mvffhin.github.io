Run to Dunkin

Jerry makes a beeline for the Dunkin' Donuts, despite knowing full well that he doesnt have the time to spare. But at this point, all he can think of is how desperately he NEEDS something to eat.

Thankfully, the Dunkin' is practically empty. Most people have already made their way to work, unlike a certain someone. Still, Jerry considers himself lucky and quickly places his order: one medium coffee and two orders of hash browns. Just enough to hold him over until lunch.

Surpisingly, his order comes out fairly quickly. Jerry snatches his breakfast off of the counter and immediately resumes his frantic sprint towards the train station.

He races down the street, darts around the corner, and practically flies into the station. He checks the arrival board.

0 MINUTES AWAY

Phew!

Jerry hops aboard as soon as the train arrives and, by some miracle even manages to find an empty seat. Finally, a little bit of good luck!

He settles in and digs into his breakfast. The hashbrowns are absolutely perfect. Granted, they're a little bit cold, but considering he hasn't eaten since yesterday, Jerry couldn't care less. He savors every last bite before moving onto his coffee, taking a few generous sips as the train carries him towards his stop.

Eventually, his train arrives. Jerry hops off the train, coffee still firmly in hand, and immediately resumes running.

8:50 AM.

Honestly? Considering the morning he's had, Jerry isnt doing too terribly.

He finally reaches his office building, rushes through the enterance, and scans his ID at the security gate. He's in!

But, of course, his luck doesn't last very long.

The elevators are out of service.

And

Jerry stares at the elevators doors in disbelief. He then remembers there's another set of elevators on the opposite side of the first floor. There's just one problem: if those elevators are out of service too, theres no convenient starewell on that side of the building like there is over here.

Jerry weighs his options.

He's running out of time, his coffee still in hand, and the clock ticking.

So, Jerry decides to...

  2. Try the other elevators
