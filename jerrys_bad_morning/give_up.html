Jerry Gives up

Jerry ultimately comes to the conclusion that the universe just simply isnt on his side today. With that, he decides there's no point in fighting fate. He turns around and walks back into his apartment, and remembers to lock the door behind him this time.

As he makes his way towards his bedroom, fully intending to crawl back into bed, something catches his eye. For the first time all morning, Jerry actually looks at himself in the mirror.

There he stands, in all of his glory, wearing his bright pink bunny rabbit pajamas.

Jerry pauses for a moment before muttering, "At least I didn't go to work in this. That would've been embarrassing."

Satisfied with this revelation, he stumbles into his bedroom, where Nappa is already comfortably sprawled across his bed. Jerry climbs in beside Nappa, pulls the blankets obver himself and promptly falls back asleep.

And then...

9:01 AM

Jerry's phone begins absolutely exploding with notifications. Email after email rolls in from his boss, each one more final than the last. Since today marks Jerry's 10th unexcused absense/lateness this month despite it only being the 11th, his boss has decided that Jerry should simply never bother showing up to work again.

Why, you ask?

Because Jerry is fired.

Jerry lets out an exhausted sigh, turns off his phone, rolls over, and goes right back to sleep.

At this point, even Nappa is probably disappointed in him.
