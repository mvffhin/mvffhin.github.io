Jerry Takes The Stairs

Jerry takes one last, long look at the elevators before muttering, "Oh, fuck it!" He springs back into action and makes a mad dash towards the stairwell. He practically tumbles down the stairs, stumbling his way through flight after flight until he finally reaches the first floor.

Without wasting anymore time, Jerry bursts through the front entrance and takes off running towards the subway station, desperately hoping to catch the next train.

But then, something catches the corner of his eye.

A Dunkin' Donuts.

"Nice!" Jerry thinks to himself.

He immediately considers stopping for a quick bite, but then remembers that doing so would cost him even more precious time. But on the other hand, he hasnt eaten anythuing since lunch yesterday, and he is STARVING.

Surely, a quick stop couldn't hurt that much... right?

After all, a man's gotta eat.

And so, Jerry comes to the brilliant conclusion that it's probably best to..
