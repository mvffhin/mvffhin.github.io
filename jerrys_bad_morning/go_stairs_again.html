Going Up The Stairs

Jerry glances at the clock.

8:50 AM...

The office is on the 16th floor.

Gulp.

Well, here goes nothing.

Jerry runs into the stairwell and starts climbing, one flight at a time. Each flight feels like an eternity, but there's no time to stop and catch his breath. He has no idea how much time has passed, but all he knows is that hes getting closer. He's almost there!

He looks up at the floor sign.

17.

...

You've got to be kidding.

Jerry grumbles to himself, drenched in sweat. He was so focused on getting upstairs that he somehow managed to walk right past his own floor.

Fantastic.

He quickly turns around, rushes down one flight of stairs, and practically launches himself down the hallway.

And then...

HE MADE IT! He actually made it!

Jerry rushes into the office, where his boss, Yoem, is waiting for him.

Yoem looks Jerry up and down. "So you actually made it. And presentable too. Good job, but you're still late. Go home. You're fired."

Jerry freezes. "WAIT, WHAT?! But I'm on time!"

He frantically points towards the clock hanging on the wall behind Yoem.

9:00 AM.

"See?! Right on the dot!"

But Yoem simply pulls out his phone and holds it up for Jerry to see... 9:05 AM.

Jerry stares at the screen, his mouth hanging open "B-b-but..."

"That clock is five minutes behind. You wouldve known that if you actually showed up, let alone on time. Now get out of here."
