Ignoring your needs

Jerry fights every instinct telling him to stop at the beautiful Dunkin' Donuts. His stomach rumbling, and he's beginning to feel a little dizzy, but none of that matters right now. What matters is getting to work on time!

So, he powers through and runs straight past the Dunkin', rounds the corner and practically flies into the subway station.

But just as Jerry makes his way down onto the platform...

THE TRAIN PULLS AWAY

Jerry watches in disbelief as the train disappears into the distance. He lets out an irritated groan and trudges further down the platform, hoping to find somewhere to sit.

Unfortunately, the only available bench is covered in trash and some unknown liquids. Lovely.

Guess he'll have to stand.

Jerry checks the arrival board.

NEXT TRAIN: 10 MINUTES.

"Ugh..."

It's already 8:15. Ten whole minutes? You've got to be kidding.

With no other choice, Jerry stands there and waits. And waits. And waits some more.

As the minutes crawl by, his attention gradually shifts away from the train schedule and towards his increasingly angry stomach. The hunger is getting worse, and so is the dizziness.

He shifts his weight, trying to steady himself, but something feels off.

His vision starts to get a little blurry.

Jerry reaches out and leans against the nearest wall hoping to regain his balance.

And then...

SPLAT.

Jerry collapses onto the filthy subway floor, completely unconscious.

Whoopsie.
