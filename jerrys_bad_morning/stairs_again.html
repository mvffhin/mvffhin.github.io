Going Up The Stairs

Jerry glances at the clock.

8:50 AM.

Oh, boy.

The office is on the 16th floor.

Gulp.

Well, here goes nothing.

Jerry throws himself into the stairwell and starts climbing, one flight at a time. Each set of stairs feels like an eternity, but there's no time to stop and catch his breath. For all he knows, he's already running out of time. All he can do is keep going. He's almost there!

Finally Jerry stops to catch his breath, absolutely convinved that he must have reached the 16th floor by now. He looks up at the floor sign.

17.

...

You've got to be kidding.

Jerry has somehow managed to climb right past his own floor. Fantastic.

Drenched in sweat and completely exhausted, he turns around and rushes back down one flight before practically launching himself out of the stairwell, and down the hallway.

And then...

HE MADE IT!

He actually made it!

Jerry bursts into the office, where his boss, Yoem is standing nearby. Yoem takes one look at Jerry, looks him up and down, and suddenly bursts into laughter.

A coworker walking past notices the commotion, stops to take a good look at Jerry, and immediately joins in.

"What?! What's so funny?!" Jerry asks, completely bewildered.

Yoem struggles to contain his laughter before finally asking "Did you even LOOk at yourself before leaving the house this morning?"

Jerry looks down.

For the first time all morning, he actually takes a good look at what he's wearing.

...

Pink. Bunny. Rabbit. Pajamas.

Jerry freezes.

He slowly brings a hand up to his face and face-palms.

Oh, you've GOT to be kidding.

And just when you think Jerry's morning couldnt possibly get any worse, Yoem decides to deliver the cherry on top

"Oh, by the way, you're fired."

Jerry's head snaps back up.

"WHAT?! But why?! I'm on time!"

He frantically points towards the clock hanging up behind Yoem.

9:00 AM

"See?! Right on the dot!"

But Yoem simply pulls out his phone and holds it up for Jerry to see.

9:05 AM

Jerry stares t the screen with his mouth hanging open.

"B-b-but..."

"That clock is five minutes behind. You would've known that if you actually showed up to work, let alone showed up on time. Now get out of here."
