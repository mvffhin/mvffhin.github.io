Taking your time

Jerry jumps out of bed and gives himself a quick stretch. He's got to get moving ASAP. But that doesnt mean he can't take a few seconds to make himself presentable.

He rushes into the bathroom, grabs his toothbrush, and starts brushing his teeth at lightning speed. As he looks down, he catches a glimpse of his outfit.

"...Yeah, I should probably put on my actual work clothes. Can't exactly show up to the office in my pajamas!"

He quickly changes into something a little more appropriate for the workplace and grabs his things before heading out.

As Jerry approachees the front door, he has a sudden realization.

Now would probably be a good time to let his boss know hes running late.

He quickly pulls out his phone and types up a lovely little email explaining his sitaution, and to his suprise he receieves a response almost instantly!

"Jerry Zeppli, today is your LAST chance. If you are late today, YOU ARE FIRED. Anway, can't wait to see you soon!"

...

Gulp.

Well, that's certainly encouraging.

Jerry puts a little more pep in his step, opens his apartment door, locks it behind him, and rushes towards the elevators.

He frantically presses the call button.

Nothing.

He presses it again.

Still nothing

One elevator is stuck on the top floor and hasn't moved an inch, while the other is completely out of service.

Jerry lets out an exhausted groan.

8:15 AM.

Oh, boy.

The clock is ticking, his boss is clearly NOT messing around, and Jerry has already encountered his first major obstacle of the day.

So... what's Jerry's next move?

  1. Keep waiting
  2. Go for the stairs
  3. Give up
